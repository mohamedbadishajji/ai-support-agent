# Order Tracking and Delays

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email. It can take
up to 24 hours for tracking information to update after the label is
created. If your tracking shows "label created" for more than 3 days with
no movement, contact support — the package may be delayed at the carrier.

Standard shipping delays are most common during holiday periods (November
through early January) — expect 2-3 extra business days during this window.

If your tracking says "delivered" but you haven't received your package,
wait 24 hours (packages are sometimes marked delivered before the driver's
final stop) and check with neighbors or your building's front desk. If it
still hasn't turned up after that, contact support with your order number
to file a lost package claim.

We are not able to redirect a package to a different address once it has
shipped — contact the carrier directly for redelivery options in that case.

# Complaints and Dispute Resolution

If you're unhappy with a resolution offered by support, you can request
escalation to a senior support specialist by replying "I'd like to escalate
this" in any support conversation.

For payment disputes (unauthorized charges, billing errors), contact us
before filing a chargeback with your bank — most billing issues can be
resolved within 48 hours directly through support, and a direct resolution
is faster than a bank dispute process.

If you experienced a service failure (e.g. repeated shipping delays,
multiple defective items), you may be eligible for a goodwill credit at our
discretion — mention this when contacting support and a specialist will
review your order history.

We aim to resolve all complaints within 3 business days. If you haven't
heard back in that time, reply to your original support thread to bump the
priority.

# Discounts, Promo Codes, and Loyalty Program

Promo codes can be applied at checkout in the "Promo Code" field. Only one
promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be combined with loyalty
points discounts.

Our loyalty program awards 1 point per $1 spent. Points can be redeemed at
checkout starting at 500 points (worth $5 off). Points expire 12 months
after they're earned if unused.

Referral codes give both the referrer and the new customer $10 off, applied
after the new customer's first order ships (to prevent fraud).

Promo codes cannot be applied retroactively to orders that have already
been placed, and expired codes cannot be honored after their listed end
date, even if the cart was created before expiration.

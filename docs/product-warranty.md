# Product Warranty and Defects

All physical products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering
defects in materials or workmanship. This does not cover normal wear and
tear, accidental damage, or misuse.

If you receive a defective item, contact support within 14 days of delivery
with photos of the defect and your order number. We'll send a replacement
at no cost, with a prepaid label to return the defective unit.

For warranty claims after 14 days but within the 1-year period, we'll
troubleshoot with you first; if the issue can't be resolved, we offer a
repair, replacement, or store credit at our discretion.

Products purchased from third-party resellers (not directly from us) are
not covered by our warranty — contact the reseller directly.

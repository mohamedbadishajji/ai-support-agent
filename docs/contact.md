# Contact and Support Hours

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM (UTC).
Live chat responses typically arrive within a few minutes during these
hours. Outside business hours, messages are queued and answered the next
business day.

For urgent issues (fraud, unauthorized charges, account security), a human
agent will prioritize your request regardless of the hour.

Email: support@example.com
Response time: within 24 hours on business days.

We do not offer phone support at this time. All support is handled through
chat and email to keep response times consistent and documented.

# Account Management

To reset your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the
email instructions. Password reset links expire after 1 hour.

To update your email or shipping address, go to Account Settings. Changes to billing
information require re-verification of your payment method.

To delete your account, contact support directly — this action is permanent and
removes all order history. We retain transaction records for 7 years as required by
law, even after account deletion.

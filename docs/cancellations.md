# Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for free within 1 hour of placing them, before they
enter processing. After that window, the order may already be packed and
cannot be cancelled — you'll need to wait for delivery and use our standard
return process instead.

Subscription orders can be cancelled anytime before the next billing date
to avoid being charged for the next cycle. Cancelling does not retroactively
refund the current billing period.

Pre-order and made-to-order items cannot be cancelled once production has
started — check the product page for the specific cancellation cutoff,
which is listed before checkout.

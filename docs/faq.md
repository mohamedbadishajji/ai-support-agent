# Frequently Asked Questions

**My tracking number isn't working yet.** Tracking numbers can take up to 24
hours to activate after your order ships. If it's been longer than that,
contact support with your order number.

**I received the wrong item.** We're sorry about that. Contact support with
your order number and a photo of what you received — we'll ship the correct
item immediately at no extra cost and provide a prepaid label to return the
wrong item.

**Can I change my shipping address after ordering?** Address changes are
only possible before the order ships. Once you see a tracking number, the
address can no longer be changed.

**Do you ship internationally?** Yes, to most countries. International
orders may be subject to customs fees, which are the customer's
responsibility and not included in the order price.

**How do I apply a promo code?** Enter it at checkout in the "Promo Code"
field before completing payment. Promo codes cannot be applied
retroactively to completed orders.

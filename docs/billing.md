# Billing and Payments

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. All payments are
processed securely and we never store your full card number on our servers.

You will be charged at the time of purchase, not at shipping. If an item in
your order is out of stock, you'll be refunded for that item within 3-5
business days and the rest of your order will ship normally.

Subscription plans renew automatically on the billing date shown in your
account. You can cancel anytime from Account Settings > Subscriptions —
cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, no
partial refunds are issued for unused time.

If you see a duplicate charge or a charge you don't recognize, contact
support immediately with your order number. Most billing disputes are
resolved within 48 hours.

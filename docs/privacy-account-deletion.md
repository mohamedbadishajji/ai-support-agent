# Data Privacy and Account Deletion

We collect your name, email, shipping address, and order history to
process purchases and provide support. We do not sell your personal data
to third parties.

You can request a copy of your personal data at any time by contacting
support — we'll provide it within 30 days as required by applicable data
protection law.

To delete your account, contact support directly and confirm your identity.
This permanently removes your saved addresses, payment methods, and
browsing preferences. Order history and transaction records are retained
for 7 years for tax and legal compliance, even after account deletion,
but are no longer linked to an active account.

You can opt out of marketing emails anytime using the unsubscribe link at
the bottom of any marketing email — this does not affect transactional
emails like order confirmations and shipping updates.

# Shipping Policy

We offer standard shipping (5-7 business days) and express shipping (1-2 business days).
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Express shipping costs $15.

Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days. You will receive a tracking
number via email once your order ships. International shipping is available to most
countries and typically takes 10-14 business days.

If your package is lost or damaged in transit, contact support within 30 days of the
expected delivery date for a replacement or refund.
